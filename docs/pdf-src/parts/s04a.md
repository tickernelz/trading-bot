
## 4.1 Decision flow

1. Freqtrade generates entry candidates from the 1-4 hour swing rule strategy.
2. Voter A (decision model, option order shuffled) and voter B (price path) answer the same question from the same state.
3. Results are combined using the median and a dispersion measure; quorum is checked.
4. Decision: allow, reduce, or skip. Quorum not met or large dispersion means skip (fail-closed).
5. The swarm never adds risk and does not touch exits or stop loss. Initial mode is shadow: logging only; veto mode comes only after the adoption criteria (4.3) are met.
