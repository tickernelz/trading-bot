# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: the owner, a senior software developer, owner of the bot and its only user at present. The owner uses the app in two situations. On their own PC, when changing configuration, setting up providers, checking voter decisions, and analysing results. On a phone (PWA), when outdoors or in the middle of the night, for a single question: is the bot safe and what is happening right now. Indonesian language. Starting capital is Rp 2 million; the bot runs 24 hours a day on their PC, initially in dry-run only.

Decision context: the direction of the theme and visual direction was delegated by the owner to the operator (full-autonomy mode, 2026-10-25). Interview answers about the feel and about liked or disliked examples were not given; the sections below are the operator's decisions, corrected by the owner along the way.

## Product Purpose

A control panel for an autonomous trading bot: to see what the bot does and why, to hold the risk limits, and to change settings safely. Not a signal-generating tool for other people and not a game. Success in the early stage: the owner trusts the bot's condition within seconds without opening the logs, is never surprised by a loss beyond the limits, and can trace every decision down to the reason and each voter's answer.

## Brand Personality

Three words: calm, honest, precise. A voice like a station departure board or a measuring instrument: it states the situation as it is, with no dramatisation, no celebration, no persuasion. The intended feeling: control that does not cause anxiety.

## Anti-references

- Binance- or Robinhood-style trading dashboards: dark background, neon green and red, candlestick charts as the centre of everything.
- Gamified broker apps: confetti, winning streaks, big profit numbers that are celebrated.
- SaaS hero-metric templates: giant numbers, small labels, gradients, identical statistic cards repeated.
- Generic AI dashboards: purple gradients, glass cards, coloured round icons, self-praising copy.
- The default look of FreqUI and Nuxt UI: the owner asked for a unique UI, not a reused standard interface.

## Design Principles

1. Risk before profit: limits, danger status, and the mode (dry-run or live) are always visible before profit numbers.
2. The bot is a measuring instrument, not a gambling machine: no celebration, no pressure to act; losses and uncertainty are stated as clearly as gains.
3. Readable within two seconds on a phone: one clear answer (safe, held, stopped) before any detail.
4. Show the reason: every decision can be opened down to the voter answers, median, spread, and quorum; no black box.
5. Do not hide uncertainty: stale data, lost connection, and unproven numbers are flagged brightly; dry-run must never look like live.
6. Calm control: dangerous actions need two steps and state the consequence in rupiah; reducing risk is always easier than raising it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Profit and loss must not be distinguished by colour alone: there is always a plus and minus sign, a direction, and text. Text contrast at least 4.5:1, including under sunlight on a phone screen. Support prefers-reduced-motion and the system text size. Touch targets large enough for one-handed use. Indonesian as the default; technical terms and product feature names are left in their original language.
